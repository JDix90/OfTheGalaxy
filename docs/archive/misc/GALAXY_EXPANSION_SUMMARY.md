## Overview

The galaxy has been expanded from 17 systems with 18 planets to **55 star systems with 88 planets**, organized across all major galactic regions from Star Wars lore.

---

## Galactic Structure

### System vs Planet Relationship

**Important:** A **Star System** contains one or more **planets**. The galaxy map displays **systems**, not individual planets.

**Example:**
- **Naboo System** contains:
  - Naboo (main planet)
  - Naboo Moon

**Gameplay Flow:**
1. Galaxy Map shows **Star Systems** (55 systems)
2. Click a system → See its **planets** in sidebar
3. Click a planet → View details and **travel** to it
4. Travel → Navigate to **Planet Surface** to explore

## Coordinate System

Systems are organized by region with appropriate coordinate ranges:

- **Core Worlds:** x: -15 to 10, y: -5 to 12 (central, densely populated)
- **Colonies:** x: -15 to 12, y: -8 to 3 (between Core and Inner Rim)
- **Inner Rim:** x: -18 to 22, y: -15 to 5 (well-developed)
- **Mid Rim:** x: 20 to 40, y: -25 to 15 (moderate development)
- **Expansion Region:** x: 42 to 48, y: -22 to 15 (less developed)
- **Outer Rim:** x: 40 to 80, y: -50 to 30 (frontier, dangerous)
- **Wild Space:** x: 85 to 90, y: -35 to 10 (unexplored)
- **Unknown Regions:** x: 95 to 105, y: -25 to 15 (mysterious, uncharted)

## Planet Types

All planets use valid types from the database schema:
- `terrestrial` - Standard rocky planets
- `gas_giant` - Large gas planets (Bespin, Yavin Prime)
- `ice` - Frozen worlds (Hoth, Mygeeto, Ilum)
- `desert` - Arid worlds (Tatooine, Jakku, Geonosis)
- `ocean` - Water worlds (Mon Cala, Kamino, Ahch-To)
- `jungle` - Forest worlds (Kashyyyk, Felucia, Dathomir)
- `volcanic` - Active volcanoes (Mustafar, Sullust)
- `barren` - Uninhabitable (Exegol)
- `urban` - City planets (Coruscant, Taris, Nar Shaddaa)

## Travel Routes

96 bidirectional hyperlanes connect systems:
- **Core Worlds:** Dense network, short travel times (2-3 hours)
- **Mid Rim:** Moderate connections (4-6 hours)
- **Outer Rim:** Sparse, longer routes (7-12 hours)
- **Wild Space/Unknown Regions:** Very long, expensive routes (12-15 hours)

Travel costs scale with distance and danger:
- Core Worlds: 100-150 credits
- Mid Rim: 200-300 credits
- Outer Rim: 300-600 credits
- Wild Space: 400-750 credits
